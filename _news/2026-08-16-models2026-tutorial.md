          Modelware founder and CEO, Dr. Maged Elaasar, will deliver the tutorial
          <em>OML Code: Turning SME Knowledge into Reusable Ontological Modeling Methodologies</em> at
          <a href="https://conf.researchr.org/track/models-2026/models-2026-tutorials#event-overview" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MODELS 2026</a>,
          the 29th International Conference on Model Driven Engineering Languages and Systems, held October 4 to 9,
          2026 in Málaga, Spain. Tutorials run as part of the satellite events, October 4 to 6.
        </p>
        <p class="text-left">
          The hardest thing to scale in engineering is not tooling, it is judgment. The methodology a subject matter
          expert carries in their head, what a good model looks like, which rules must hold, how the pieces connect,
          usually stays with that expert. The tutorial is about writing it down in a form a machine can act on.
        </p>
        <p class="text-left">
          Participants will see how <a href="/oml-code">OML Code</a> lets an expert codify a methodology as
          <a href="/oml-code/capabilities/declarative-methodology">declarative, executable knowledge</a>:
          <a href="/oml-code/capabilities/formal-knowledge-representation">formal ontologies</a> that define the
          concepts and rules of a discipline, <a href="/oml-code/capabilities/automated-reasoning">automated
          reasoning</a> that checks models against them continuously, and a methodology that can then be reused across
          teams and projects rather than re-explained.
        </p>
        <p class="text-left">
          Attending MODELS 2026? <a href="/contact.html">Reach out</a> to meet with us in Málaga.
