        <p>
          Modelware is co-organizing the second edition of the
          <a href="https://opencaesar.io/events/onto-Nexus-Workshop-2026" target="_blank" rel="noopener">onto:Nexus Workshop</a>,
          formally the International Workshop on Ontological Modeling and Analysis. It takes place on Tuesday,
          October 6, 2026 in Málaga, Spain, co-located with
          <a href="https://conf.researchr.org/home/models-2026" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MODELS 2026</a>.
        </p>
        <p>
          We are joined on the organizing committee by Dr. Bentley Oakes (Polytechnique Montréal), Dr. Wahab
          Hamou-Lhadj (Concordia University), Dr. Mohammad Hamdaqa (Polytechnique Montréal), and Dr. Eduard Kamburjan
          (IT University of Copenhagen).
        </p>
        <p>
          The workshop is a venue for peer-reviewed work on ontological modeling and analysis applied to model-based
          software and systems engineering: formal methods for rigor, DevOps practices for agility, and AI techniques
          for automation. Those three threads are the same ones that
          <a href="/oml-code">OML Code</a> is built on, which is why this community matters to us.
        </p>

        <h4 class="mt-5 mb-3">The program</h4>
        <p>
          The workshop runs a full day of talks and discussion, anchored by a keynote from Prof. Andreas Harth
          (Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg and Fraunhofer IIS), <em>Towards Unified Process Descriptions for Monitoring, Executing,
          Verifying &amp; Governing Behaviour</em>.
        </p>
        <p>Three accepted papers will be presented:</p>
        <ul class="mb-4">
          <li class="mb-2">
            <em>Orchestration Graph: From Knowledge Graphs to Executable Analysis Workflows with openCAESAR</em>,
            by Yuta Nakajima, Yutaka Komatsu, and J. Steven Jenkins
          </li>
          <li class="mb-2">
            <em>Representing Engineered Redundancy with the Ontological Modeling Language: A Space Systems Case
            Study</em>, by Sophia Salas Cordero, Mark Chodas, Michel D. Ingham, and Rob Vingerhoeds
          </li>
          <li class="mb-2">
            <em>From Asset Administration Shells to Knowledge Graphs: Rule-Based Semantic Lifting for Cyber-Physical
            Systems</em>, by Harish Kumar Pakala, Bianca Wiesmayr, and Christian Diedrich
          </li>
        </ul>
        <p>
          The program also includes a round table on <em>How to leverage neurosymbolic AI in MBSE?</em>, a question we have
          taken up ourselves in
          <a href="/the-case-for-neurosymbolic-systems-engineering">The Case for Neurosymbolic Systems Engineering</a>.
        </p>
        <p>
          Accepted papers will be published by IEEE in the joint workshop proceedings.
        </p>
