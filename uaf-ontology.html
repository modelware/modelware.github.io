The OMG Unified Architecture Framework (UAF) unifies the defense architecture frameworks (DoDAF, MODAF, NAF, DNDAF) into a single grid of viewpoints and aspects. It is used to plan by capability, trace strategy down to fielded resources, and reason about system-of-systems architectures.

In this series we rendered the UAF Domain Metamodel (DMM) as a faithful, platform-neutral ontology on OML Code, and used it to cross-check every element and relationship against the normative UAF Modeling Language (UAFML) profile. Each part reads UAF in light of its own stated objectives, and shows what an ontological implementation adds: automated reasoning that checks the specification, queries that compute views, and AI grounded in a formal model.

Key terms

  • UAF: the Unified Architecture Framework, the OMG standard for enterprise and system-of-systems architecture, organized as a grid of viewpoints and aspects.
  • UAF DMM: the Domain Metamodel, UAF's platform-independent definition of its concepts and relationships. This is the part we render as an ontology.
  • UAFML: the UAF Modeling Language, the SysML profile that realizes the DMM in SysML tools.
  • OML: the Ontological Modeling Language, a description-logic modeling language designed for engineering, and the language of OML Code.
